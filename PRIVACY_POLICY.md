# VEGA STING — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 29 September 2026
**Developer:** VIJAYKRSHA.ONLINE
**Package Name:** `com.vega.sting`
**Document Version:** 1.1

---

## 1. Privacy Commitment

VEGA STING is built on the principle that your recordings belong entirely to you. The application is designed so that audio and video recordings captured through it remain on your device unless you personally choose to share, export, or delete them. The developer does not receive, access, view, upload, or retain copies of your recordings.

## 2. Purpose of This Application

VEGA STING is a video and audio recording application. It enables you to record audio and video, manage recordings locally, play them back, and organise them into a trash system for temporary recovery. The application is intended for lawful personal, professional, journalistic, and documentary use.

## 3. User Control

You have complete control over your recordings. You can start, stop, pause, resume, play, export, share, and permanently delete recordings at any time. The application does not impose retention limits, watermarks, hidden copies, or background uploads. If you delete a recording, it is removed from the application.

## 4. Privacy of Recordings

All recordings are stored locally on your device. Recordings are not uploaded to any server, cloud platform, or third-party service. Recordings are not transmitted to the developer. Recordings are not visible to the developer, its personnel, or any third party.

## 5. No Automatic Data Transmission to Authorities

The application does not automatically transmit your recordings, metadata, or any other content to law enforcement agencies, government bodies, or any other authority. The developer has no mechanism to receive such data from the application and does not request it.

## 6. Data Sharing and Deletion

The application does not share your recordings with any third party. You may choose to share recordings using Android's built-in system sharing features, in which case the destination application or service you select receives the file. This sharing is initiated entirely by you. If you delete a recording or clear the application's data, the recordings are removed.

## 7. No Developer Access to Recordings

The developer has no access to your recordings. The application contains no feature that transmits, uploads, or shares recordings with the developer or any third party. The developer cannot view, download, retrieve, or delete your recordings.

## 8. Network Connectivity

The application uses network connectivity for one purpose only: to check for application updates from a public GitHub Releases repository. During an update check, the application contacts the GitHub API to retrieve version information. If an update is available, the application downloads the update file (an APK) from GitHub. No recording content, personal data, or identifying information is ever transmitted over the network.

## 9. Device and Camera Information

To ensure compatibility and stability, the application may read device and camera capability information such as supported video resolutions, frame rates, encoder capabilities, and device model characteristics. This information is processed locally on your device to determine appropriate recording settings. It is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## 10. Session Information and Local Telemetry

The application may record local session telemetry, such as the outcome and error conditions of a recording session, stored only on your device for the purpose of diagnostics and troubleshooting. This information is stored locally, is not linked to any external identity, and is not transmitted to the developer. You may clear it by clearing the application's data.

## 11. Permissions

The application requests the following permissions solely to perform the functions you choose to use:

- **Camera** — to capture video.
- **Microphone** — to capture audio.
- **Storage and media permissions** — to save, read, and play back recordings and to import from external storage.
- **Notification and foreground service permissions** — to sustain recording while the screen is off and to show an ongoing recording indicator.
- **Network access** — to check for updates as described in Section 8.

Each permission is requested at the point where it is needed. You may deny or revoke any permission, which will limit the corresponding functionality.

## 12. No Advertising

The application contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party marketing components. Your recordings and your data are not used for advertising purposes.

## 13. No Third-Party Analytics or AI Data Processing

The application contains no analytics SDKs, no tracking libraries, and no third-party artificial intelligence or machine learning services that process your recordings or personal data. There is no telemetry sent to any third party.

## 14. Lawful Use

The application is a recording tool. It is intended for lawful use only. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the application, and any recording you make, complies with all applicable laws, including laws relating to privacy, surveillance, workplace monitoring, and consent of the people being recorded.

## 15. Recording in Public and Private Spaces

When recording in public places, private property, or any location where individuals may have a reasonable expectation of privacy, it is your responsibility to comply with applicable privacy and consent laws. The application does not provide any guidance on the permissibility of a particular recording; you must determine this independently.

## 17. Third-Party Software

The application may incorporate third-party open-source software components. These components are used under their own licences. Such components do not receive, process, or transmit your recordings or personal data.

## 18. Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The application is designed with data minimisation and local processing as core principles, consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (as applicable in India). Specifically:

- **Data minimisation** — the application does not collect personal data about you.
- **Local processing** — recordings are processed and stored on your device.
- **No data brokers** — your data is not sold, rented, or traded.
- **No unsolicited communication** — the application does not send you marketing or promotional messages.
- **Your rights** — you have the right to access, correct, complete, update, and erase your data, and to withdraw consent. In this application, you exercise these rights directly by managing your recordings within the application and by clearing the application's data.

You have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with the Act if you believe your data has been mishandled. As the developer holds no copy of your data, the developer cannot retrieve, correct, or erase data held on your behalf; you exercise these rights by controlling the data on your device.

## 19. Local Recordings and Government Access

Recordings stored on your device may be accessed by law enforcement or other authorities only through lawful means, such as a valid warrant or court order, and in accordance with applicable law. The developer has no access to these recordings and cannot provide them to any authority, because the developer does not possess them.

## 20. External Storage

## 21. Data Security

The developer implements reasonable safeguards within the application, but no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
